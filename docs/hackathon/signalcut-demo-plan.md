# SignalCut Demo Plan

## Best Track Positioning

SignalCut should be positioned for:

- Hera: AI Agents for Video Generation
- Peec AI: 0 -> 1 AI Marketer
- Overall finalist/wildcard through multi-partner execution

The strongest pitch is not "we generate videos." It is:

> SignalCut is an AI marketer that finds where AI search recommends your competitor, explains the evidence gap, and turns that gap into a source-backed Hera video campaign.

## Partner Technologies To Mention

- Peec AI: visibility gap and competitor context
- Google DeepMind: campaign reasoning and creative strategy
- Tavily: source evidence and claim grounding
- Hera: motion video job creation

## Best Demo Example

Use the hackathon-provided Attio example:

- Brand: Attio
- Competitors: Salesforce, HubSpot
- Prompt gap: best CRM for agencies with custom onboarding workflows
- Problem: AI answers default to Salesforce and HubSpot even though Attio has stronger workflow flexibility proof.
- Output: source-backed campaign brief, three editorial video variants, Hera job, export pack.

## Two-Minute Recording Script

0:00-0:12

AI search is becoming a distribution channel. The problem is that early-stage brands can see visibility gaps in tools like Peec, but they still do not know what to create next.

0:12-0:25

This is SignalCut. It turns a Peec visibility gap into a source-backed video campaign, using Peec AI, Google DeepMind, Tavily, and Hera.

0:25-0:45

For the demo, I am using Attio versus Salesforce and HubSpot. The target prompt is "best CRM for agencies with custom onboarding workflows." SignalCut ranks this as a high-value competitor-dominance gap.

0:45-1:05

When I generate a campaign, SignalCut pulls source evidence and keeps risk labels next to each claim. That matters because the agent should not invent competitor attacks or unsupported marketing claims.

1:05-1:30

The agent then makes an editorial decision. It writes the hook, core claim, CTA, and three different video angles. This is where it acts like a creative strategist, not just a prompt wrapper.

1:30-1:48

Here is the Hera-ready output. SignalCut sends the selected concept to Hera and returns the queued video project, while also keeping the prompt, script, caption, and source list exportable.

1:48-2:00

The result is a repeatable AI marketing loop: Peec gap, evidence, strategy, Hera video, campaign export. Small brands can turn AI-search weakness into distribution assets in minutes.

## Five-Minute Finalist Pitch Shape

1. Open with the insight: AI answers are now a distribution surface.
2. Show the Attio gap and explain why the gap matters commercially.
3. Show SignalCut ranking the opportunity.
4. Show evidence and risk controls.
5. Show creative brief and the three editorial variants.
6. Show the Hera job and export pack.
7. Close with why this helps early-stage brands fight incumbents.
